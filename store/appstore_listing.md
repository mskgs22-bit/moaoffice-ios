**Description:**
MoaOffice opens all your everyday documents in one simple app — completely offline.

• PDF & AI: smooth reading, highlight, underline and strikethrough, save annotations
• HWP & HWPX (Hangul): read Korean word-processor documents with paragraphs and tables
• Export HWP/HWPX to PDF in one tap
• Word (DOC, DOCX), Excel (XLS, XLSX) and CSV previews
• Photoshop PSD/PSB images
• Print any document with AirPrint
• Open files from the Files app, Mail, KakaoTalk or any app with "Open in"

Private by design: your files never leave your iPhone. No account, no upload.

MoaOffice is free with ads. Remove ads anytime with a monthly or yearly subscription.
